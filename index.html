AI DGT Marketing

AI DGT Marketing is a digital marketing studio for companies. A company gives it a text, a link, a recording or a file, and it makes videos, carousels and captions for the company's social accounts.

A person can bring a video, a PDF or pictures from their own Google Drive. The product opens Google's own file window, and it can read only the files the person picks there.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026.

What we read from Google

AI DGT Marketing asks Google for one permission: drive.file. With it, the product sees only the files a person picks in Google's own file window. It cannot list, open or change any other file in that person's Google Drive.

What we do with it

  • A picked file is copied into the private file store of the company the person works for, and used only to make that company's content.
  • One company's files and Google connection are never shown or given to another company.
  • The Google connection is kept encrypted on our server. It is never written in logs and never sent to anyone else.
  • We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How to remove it

A company can disconnect Google Drive in the product at any time, which deletes the stored connection. A person can also remove the access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. A company can ask for its files and data to be deleted.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

What we read from Google

AI DGT Marketing asks Google for one permission: drive.file. With it, the product sees only the files a person picks in Google's own file window. It cannot list, open or change any other file in that person's Google Drive.

What we do with it

  • A picked file is copied into the private file store of the company the person works for, and used only to make that company's content.
  • One company's files and Google connection are never shown or given to another company.
  • The Google connection is kept encrypted on our server. It is never written in logs and never sent to anyone else.
  • We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How to remove it

A company can disconnect Google Drive in the product at any time, which deletes the stored connection. A person can also remove the access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. A company can ask for its files and data to be deleted.

What we read from Meta (Instagram)

A company connects its own Instagram professional account by signing in with Facebook. With that sign-in, AI DGT Marketing reads and does only this:

  • The company's own posts and numbers: its posts and videos (picture, caption, link, kind), their numbers, the account's daily reach and followers, the hours its followers are online, and its followers by city, age and gender. Meta gives these as counts over many people, never as a list of people.
  • Publishing: the posts the company approves in the product are sent to its own account.
  • Comments on its posts: only when the company turns on the automatic reply. When someone comments the company's chosen word, the product reads the comment once to check for the word, and sends that person one private message with the link they asked for. The comment's text is not kept. The commenter's name and profile are never read or kept. What is kept is the comment's number, the post's number, the word, the time, and whether the message went out. These records are deleted after 30 days.
  • Ads: the company's ad accounts, and, only when a person in the company presses to boost a post, that boost and its state.

What we do with it

  • Everything we read is used only to show the company its own results, to publish for it, and to send the link a commenter asked for.
  • One company's Instagram data and connection are never shown or given to another company.
  • The Instagram connection is kept encrypted on our server. It is never written in logs and never sent to anyone else.
  • Instagram data is never sent to an AI model. If this changes, for example to let the AI learn from the company's own results, this page will say so first.
  • We do not sell Meta user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

How long we keep it

The connection is kept until the company disconnects the account. The company's posts and numbers are kept while it uses the product, because Meta keeps little history. The reply records are kept for 30 days.

How to remove it

A company can disconnect Instagram in the product at any time, which deletes the stored connection at once. A person can also remove our access in Instagram (Settings › Website permissions › Apps and websites) or on Facebook (Settings › Business integrations). To delete all of a company's data, or the record of one comment, see Data deletion.

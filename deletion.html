Data deletion · AI DGT Marketing

To remove your Instagram account from AI DGT Marketing: disconnect it in the product, or remove "AI DGT Marketing" in Instagram (Settings › Website permissions › Apps and websites) or on Facebook (Settings › Business integrations). The stored connection is deleted, and no more data is read.

To delete all the data we hold about your account: write to mnserra@gmail.com with the Instagram account's name. Within 30 days we delete its posts, numbers, reply records and connection, and confirm to you by e-mail.

If you commented on a company's post and got a message from us: we never kept your comment's text or your name. The record of the reply (a number, the word and the time) is deleted by itself after 30 days. To have it deleted sooner, write to the same address with the post and the day.
